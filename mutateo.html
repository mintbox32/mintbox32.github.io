Project

Mutateo

A creature-driven concept piece exploring mutation, threat, and a gritty post-human visual language.

Creature Design Concept Art Texturing

Overview

Mutateo explores the evolution of a hostile creature form through anatomy, surface detail, and visual distortion. The brief encouraged a mix of biomechanical complexity and grotesque elegance, creating a memorable creature identity within a dangerous world.

Production

Focus was placed on readability, silhouette, and variation across materials and forms. The project was a strong study in balancing believable mutation with striking design choices that read clearly in a game context.
